Occupant IDs debris_field, tractor_lane, occlusion_screen. All use one ordinary free slot on an intact owned wedge and energy purchase; no power demand. Terrain effects are inactive when supporting wedge HP is zero; retained objects reactivate on repair; full collapse removes contents as existing total-loss behavior. No individual terrain HP/damage. Tractor record includes direction: int, exactly -1 (counterclockwise) or +1 (clockwise), wraps wedges 1/12. At most one Tractor per wedge. Other terrain can share a wedge in different available slots; duplicate debris/identical shadows add no extra effect. State validation rejects malformed direction or multiple lanes.
