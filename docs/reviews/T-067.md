## Delivered behavior

The main scene is `scenes/application.tscn`, a fixed 1440 x 810 game viewport scaled proportionally inside the window. Project minimum is 1280 x 720; 1920 x 1080, fullscreen and non-16:9 letterboxing are verified. Start/configuration, shop, settings, reference, pause, results and the 11-step practice tutorial share the dark industrial housing, amber primary action and local Barlow typography. Fixed footers keep primary actions reachable at 130% scale. Locked loadouts are labeled with shop cost and disabled before Start; locked tools remain visibly explained and are blocked at selection/action time.

RunRules prepares the single simulation and complete seven-kind roster. ProfileStore must commit begin before play, and reward settlement before results permit leaving/retrying. Controller adopts the saved revision after every commit. Results show human reward components, earned/saved amount and disable redundant saving. Shop upgrade rows visibly show current level and per-level bonus. Settings persist immediately; explicit backup and unknown/stale-lock recovery preserve failures until the user retries. Lock notices block underlying input.

Q/Ring Plate purchases one complete next ring immediately; echoed key events cannot repeat. Other building tools remain selected for distinct world clicks; abilities remain one-shot. G targets Relay rebuilding. Selected Relay HP and authoritative power demand/output are visible; the minimap shows brownout after wedge fills with a non-color slash cue. Existing pan/zoom, minimap focus, compact tabs and art preview controls remain intact.

Seven enemy kinds retain distinct graybox markers and counter reference. New encounter notices are bounded by kind. Committed weapon hits produce capped 256 transient traces, EMP pulse or Lance line; settings disable them or shorten their duration. Pause freezes them; the last expired effect queues redraw even after simulation ends. Endpoints use pre-tick target positions, so these are cosmetic event feedback rather than projectile simulation. Normal markers retain existing batching; elite shapes use immediate drawing. No gameplay delays, damage or API changes were introduced.

Practice explicitly uses 10000 training energy, quiet normal pressure and disabled scheduled elites. It requires actual placement, a weapon kill, expansion, Wall, authentic collapse through a deliberately opened ring-2 breach, reclaim, repair against modified max HP, Relay rebuild, solar cast, minimap focus and reference acknowledgement. Practice completion persists and awards no campaign credits. Replay/exit are available; tutorial card hides behind pause/settings/results.

## Recorder and dependencies

T070 application hooks use Sol's RunRecorder helper. Each durable begin creates a local user://runs JSONL trace with build/profile snapshots, choices and progression modifiers. Actual command wrappers record one outcome at the exact committed simulation._ticks (root-approved diagnostic access), including rejected commands, plus pause/resume/error/end. Tutorial fixture steps are explicitly diagnostic markers. Recording failures stay visible and do not block valid gameplay; incomplete traces never become resume files. See T070 helper report for parser/build-manifest guarantees. Packaged native input driver is owned by Sol.

Unmodified Barlow Regular/SemiBold and Barlow Semi Condensed SemiBold come from official Google Fonts, with SIL OFL1.1 and exact source/SHA256 provenance under assets/ui/fonts/barlow. No Windows system font or movie asset is redistributed. Design tokens are in [T-064-ui-plan.md](T-064-ui-plan.md); current player controls are in [CONTROLS.md](../CONTROLS.md).
