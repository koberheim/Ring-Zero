## Plan and critique before implementation

The frontend-design skill and all four local mockups were read. The approved comparison pair is industrial and painterly. Compact tokens: space #05070B, metal #39414B, rim #9CA7AF, warm light #D68D42, danger #E7654B and text #E6E8EC. Existing UI font and control theme keep the player's comparison focused on the board and star. Build controls stay left; health overview stays upper right; comparison controls occupy a bounded rectangle below it.

The central star is the expressive focus. Industrial derives its close panel layers, radial struts, fasteners and turret silhouettes from the industrial reference; painterly derives quiet structural silhouettes, warm local illumination and restrained violet atmosphere from the painterly reference. The establishing image informs strategic simplification. The schematic reference reinforces clear input boundaries, without selecting its different visual style.

Pre-build critique rejected stretching a mockup over interactive geometry, full-screen fire/noise and invented gaps/buildings: decoration must derive from authoritative wedge polygons and actual occupants. All owned ground retains a subtle continuous footprint. Close detail must scale with the world; strategic scale uses minimum-size markers and selected-only labels.

## Implementation

`scenes/art_preview.tscn` extends the real live presentation, including unbounded expansion, normal funding/pressure, existing purchase controls, Menu/loss/Retry and camera controls. Main scene is unchanged. The only shared-code change is the approved `_draw_board()` extraction: the base draw body is unchanged, and inherited live wall/Tunneler warning rendering still runs afterward.

All art is procedural. A bounded four-octave GL-compatible shader gives the actual-radius core multi-scale surface granulation with slow radius-dependent angular advection, limb light and sparse evolving wisps. Explicit phase replaces engine TIME, so motion off and pause freeze cleanly. Palette variants change colors only. Enemy markers remain the existing batched meshes, above the star/atmosphere. No enemy scene nodes are added.

A separate quiet space shader and core-anchored graded atmosphere support painterly; warm vertex light affects its dark bands. Industrial uses shaded subdivisions, close-only struts/fasteners and silhouettes for real occupants. Decorative geometry and static colors are cached by grid identity; slot positions are lazy cached by cell/slot/count. Strategic rendering skips empty-slot geometry unless selecting/building, and close rendering skips completely offscreen cells. These caches hold presentation derivations only.
