# T-027 delivery — Tunneler runtime

Implemented approved Tunneler admission, underground travel, emergence, inside attack, and subsequent normal routing. Source/tests are frozen for Astra review; the exclusive Godot window is closed. Changed only `src/gameplay/live_simulation.gd`, `src/gameplay/weapon_rules.gd`, their two gameplay test files, and this report. No profile, core, presentation, normal digest baseline, or pure Tunneler helper changes.

`LiveSimulation.create(profile, limit, include_tunnelers = false)` retains the previous default. The instance exposes `tunnelers_enabled` and `skipped_tunneler_arrivals`; `skipped_arrivals` remains the normal-only counter. Both due streams merge chronologically before movement, with eligible Tunnelers winning exact-time ties. Admissions preserve lifetime-ID order and share pool capacity. Eligibility scans inspect at most twelve bearings per pending candidate against frozen admission state; after capacity fills, remaining counts are skipped arithmetically. Missing-inner arrivals never consume slots or create backlog. Counter errors and descriptor validation remain atomic.

Tunneler snapshots add `kind`, `phase`, `targetable`, `start_position`, `destination`, `surface_cell`, `burrow_elapsed_seconds`, and `burrow_duration_seconds` to existing target fields. Normal records and events keep their previous shape. `phase` progresses through `burrowing`, `surface_attack`, and `roaming`; `targetable` is false only underground. Snapshot consumers can derive countdown directly from duration minus elapsed. Public snapshots and public WeaponRules results independently clone all three exposed PolarPositions. Private staging borrows their read-only references and replaces moved positions; phase/timing/HP and new admissions commit only after successful downstream rules. A failed combat tick may consume admission IDs but leaves old payloads, positions, destination, phase, events, counters, time, and navigation unchanged.

Burrow speed uses immutable endpoint distance divided by configured duration through PolarMotion. New arrivals receive only their post-arrival fraction of the tick; underground entities neither attack nor receive weapon hits. Exact emergence locks position to destination and exposes the entity to that tick's weapon phase. Leftover time attacks the intact destination from inside, bypassing its outer-edge wall. Underfoot destruction uses the existing shared destruction/collapse routine and stops that attacker for the tick. An inactive wall record remains on a broken supporting wedge, as approved; it no longer blocks. If the target disappeared, or once it breaks, the entity follows existing normal navigation and core pressure.

Lifecycle validation checks types, phases, targetability, duration/elapsed bounds, historical endpoint bounds, same bearing, surface-cell correspondence, finite stats/speed, and current progress. Locked endpoints retain exact descriptor shape; reconstructed current positions use `PolarMotion.RADIAL_TOLERANCE` (1e-8 world units) and `ANGULAR_TOLERANCE` (1e-12 turns). Scheduled-time subtraction can create an unrepresentable positive tail at initial admission. Only while elapsed burrow time is zero, an initial displacement no larger than radial tolerance may be omitted while its time is consumed. This can occur at most once: every subsequent positive-time call retains PolarMotion behavior and failure semantics. A 1e9-second duration fixture verifies slow representable motion across twenty ticks without accumulating lost displacement. Duration endpoint subtraction absorbs only 1e-12 seconds of roundoff.
