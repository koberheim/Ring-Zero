## Exact correction

`live_simulation.gd:522` tracks whether the current machine has already spent positive motion time in this tick. At `:571-581`, only a failed PolarMotion call can consume the residual, and only when all conditions hold:

- Positive motion was already completed in this call; initial/full-tick unsupported motion is not forgiven.
- Remaining time is at most `FIXED_STEP * 1e-12` (approximately1.667e-14 seconds).
- Remaining physical distance is at most existing `PolarMotion.RADIAL_TOLERANCE` (1e-8world units).
- Its angular equivalent is at most existing `PolarMotion.ANGULAR_TOLERANCE` (1e-12turns).
- The sole error is exactly angular or radial progress too small to represent.

That bounded residual ends this machine's current movement loop, preserving the already installed next waypoint. It does not change global elapsed time, skip other actors, fabricate displacement, alter cooldowns or suppress any other error. Every representable PolarMotion result remains untouched. `:586` records positive time after successful motion; partial travel exits normally, so the residual branch occurs only after a completed endpoint/temporary boundary. Existing finite node/boundary traversal limits and downstream stage/commit rollback remain unchanged.
