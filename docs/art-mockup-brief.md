# RING ZERO — Mockup Brief

**Purpose:** produce a small set of review mockups that test the *risky* decisions in D-120 and D-123, before any production art is generated (pipeline stage 0).
**Self-contained:** everything needed is in this file. Source of truth remains [art-direction-style-guide.md](art-direction-style-guide.md); this is the extract shaped for generation.
**These are review images, not assets.** They do not follow the production pipeline's flat-lighting or isolated-subject rules — they are scenes, meant to be looked at and judged.

---

## The look, in one paragraph

A thin, skeletal wheel of working industrial machinery orbiting a star it cages, seen from almost directly overhead — tilted 20° from vertical, fixed orientation, never rotated. Concentric thin structural bands with radial truss spokes, mostly open void between them. Weathered grey-steel hardware with panel lines, rivets and hazard markings, lit warm from the star at the centre and by its own amber working lamps. Space is near-black; the star's corona bleeds outward through the gaps and fades to dark starfield at the rim. A swarm of small dark machines approaches from one side.

## Rules that must hold in every image

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Value structure** | Lit structure, dark ground. Never dark silhouette structure on a bright field |
| **Camera** | 20° from vertical — nearly overhead. Rings are near-circular, only ~6% elliptical |
| **Structure is thin** | Narrow bands + spokes. **Not** wide solid plates or a filled disc |
| **Void dominates** | Most area between rings is empty — that's where turrets project and machines fly |
| **Star is circular** | It is a sphere; never elliptical, regardless of tilt |
| **Warm = us** | Star and player lights are amber/warm white |
| **Cold = them** | Machine accents are cyan / hard white — never warm |
| **Turrets face outward** | Mounted on bands, projecting into the gap ahead |
| **Depth** | Bands and spokes are beams and girders with visible side faces, not flat lines |

**Never:** rotating camera, perspective vanishing points, dark-structure-on-bright-field, solid filled disc, red/orange machine glows, visible text or UI.

## Shot list — four images

### Shot 1 — Strategic view *(the most important one)*
**Tests:** does thin structure + silhouette variation + ring banding actually solve the flat grey disc that T-049 produced?

Whole fortress from far out: 8–10 concentric thin rings around a small bright star. Radial spokes on every wedge line. Pylons, antenna masts and docking spurs breaking the outline so the fortress reads as a recognisable *shape*, not nested circles. Inner rings scorched and hot-toned, outer rings pale, cold and sparse. One or two wedges visibly missing — gaps in the wheel. Swarm as a dark haze approaching from the upper right.

**Judge it by:** can you tell the rings apart? Does it have a silhouette you'd recognise? Is it obviously *not* a grey disc?

---

### Shot 2 — Close on a ring segment
**Tests:** does the 20° tilt read? Do bands and spokes gain real weight from depth?

Two adjacent ring bands filling the frame, one behind the other, with the open void gap between them. Visible band thickness — top face and side face. Truss spokes as open girders. Four or five turrets mounted along the near band facing outward: a multi-barrel flak cluster, a long heavy rail, a coil dish, a small twin mount — each on the same armoured mounting base. Amber working lamps and lit seams along the bands. Warm star light from below frame, cold black above.

**Judge it by:** do the turrets read as different weapons at a glance? Does the structure feel like built hardware rather than drawn lines? Is 20° enough tilt to feel dimensional — or does it look flat?

---

### Shot 3 — Signature image
**Tests:** the whole identity in one frame, and whether corona bleed delivers the painterly half.

Mid-range: large dominant star at centre, three or four thin rings crowding close around it, warm corona light spilling through every gap and rim-lighting all inward-facing edges, fading to dark starfield at the outer rim. Swarm streaming in from one side. Some structure damaged, one wedge gone.

**Judge it by:** is this the image you'd put on the store page? Does it feel like industrial hardware *and* awe at once, or has one swallowed the other?

---

### Shot 4 — Swarm legibility
**Tests:** dark silhouettes against void, and whether cold elite accents actually pop.

Close-mid on one ring segment under attack. Hundreds of small dark machines — blunt harvester drones with intake maws, grinder heads and solar vanes, no glow at all. Among them three larger elites carrying **cold cyan** accent lights: one low with a drill head, one with an armoured ram prow, one huge and anchored. Turrets firing outward into them.

**Judge it by:** can you find the elites instantly? Do the standard machines read as a mass without becoming visual noise? Do cold accents hold up against the warm star?

## Variants worth generating

Cheap to produce and each answers a real open question:

- **Shot 1 at 20° vs. true top-down** — is the tilt worth having at strategic zoom at all?
- **Shot 3 with a red giant vs. white star** — do cold machine accents survive every star skin, as D-120 requires?
- **Shot 2 with thicker bands** — confirms thin is right, or reveals it isn't.

---

## What a mockup can't settle

These images test *composition and identity*. They cannot validate: readability during motion, whether a thousand machines stay legible at real density, whether the tilt breaks slot-picking precision, or whether rotated single sprites lean badly at the far bearings. Those fail only in engine, which is why the pipeline's review gate (§6) exists and why T-078's five-asset slice is the real proving step.

Treat approval here as "the direction is right", not "the art is settled".
